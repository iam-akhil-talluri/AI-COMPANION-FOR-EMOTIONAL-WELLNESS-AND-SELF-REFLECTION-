
 
 
# Capstone Project P05-01: AI COMPANION FOR EMOTIONAL WELLNESS & SELF-REFLECTION

A safety-aware, non-clinical AI companion for reflective emotional support with controlled safety escalation and privacy-aware session management.

## Overview

The AI Emotional Wellness Companion is a safety-aware, non-clinical conversational application designed to provide reflective emotional support while maintaining clear boundaries around professional care.

The system is designed to:

* Provide brief, warm, reflective conversational support
* Screen user messages for potential safety concerns
* Provide predefined escalation responses and reviewed crisis resources
* Provide guided coping tools such as breathing, journaling, and grounding
* Maintain session-based conversation history
* Support privacy-aware analytics and operational logging
* Prevent raw conversation content from being exposed through the analytics interface

> **Important:** The application is not intended to replace professional therapy, medical care, diagnosis, or treatment.
