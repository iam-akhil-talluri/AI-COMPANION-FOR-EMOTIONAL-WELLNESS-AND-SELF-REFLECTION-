# Coping with Exam and Study Stress

Some pressure before exams is normal, but it can build up into worry that makes studying harder. Breaking revision into small, specific tasks makes the workload feel more manageable.

Helpful habits include studying in focused blocks of 25 to 50 minutes with short breaks, keeping a regular sleep schedule, eating regular meals, and moving your body each day.

When worries spiral, it can help to write them down and ask: Is this something I can act on now? If yes, choose one small step. If not, try setting the worry aside for a planned worry time later.

Talking to a teacher, tutor, classmate, or student support service about feeling overwhelmed is a sign of strength, not weakness.
