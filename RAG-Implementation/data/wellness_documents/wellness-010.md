# Managing Relationship Conflict

Disagreements are a normal part of close relationships. How a conflict is handled often matters more than the disagreement itself.

Helpful approaches include taking a short break if emotions are running high, using I statements such as I felt hurt when this happened instead of you always, and listening to understand rather than to reply.

It can help to reflect on what you need from the conversation: to be heard, to find a solution, or to repair the connection.

If a relationship involves fear, control, or harm, that is not ordinary conflict, and reaching out for support is important.
