# Sleep and Emotional Wellbeing

Sleep and mood affect each other: stress can make it hard to sleep, and poor sleep can make emotions feel more intense.

Habits that support sleep include going to bed and waking at similar times each day, limiting screens and caffeine in the hours before bed, and keeping the bedroom dark and quiet.

If your mind is busy at night, try writing a short list of tomorrow's tasks or worries before bed so they feel held somewhere other than your head.

Ongoing sleep problems are worth mentioning to a doctor.
