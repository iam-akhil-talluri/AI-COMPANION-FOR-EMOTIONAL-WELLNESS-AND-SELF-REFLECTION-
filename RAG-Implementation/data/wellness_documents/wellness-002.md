# 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Grounding exercises bring attention back to the present moment when thoughts are racing or feelings are overwhelming.

The 5-4-3-2-1 technique: notice five things you can see, four things you can touch, three things you can hear, two things you can smell, and one thing you can taste. Saying them out loud or writing them down can make the exercise more effective.

Other simple grounding ideas: press your feet into the floor and notice the contact, hold something cold, or describe an object in the room in detail.
