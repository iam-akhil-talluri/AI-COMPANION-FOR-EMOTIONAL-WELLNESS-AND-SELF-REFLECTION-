# Journaling Prompts for Self-Reflection

Writing about thoughts and feelings can help make sense of them and notice patterns over time. There is no right way to journal; a few honest sentences are enough.

Prompts to try: What is taking up most of my mental energy right now? What emotion am I feeling, and where do I notice it in my body? What is one thing that went okay today? What would I say to a friend who was feeling the way I feel?

A short daily check-in can be as simple as rating your mood from 1 to 10 and writing one sentence about why.
