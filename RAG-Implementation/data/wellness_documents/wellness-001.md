# Slow Breathing for Stress

When stress or anxiety rises, breathing often becomes fast and shallow. Slowing the breath down, especially making the out-breath longer than the in-breath, can help the body settle.

Box breathing: breathe in through the nose for a count of four, hold for four, breathe out slowly for four, and hold for four. Repeat for one to three minutes.

Extended exhale: breathe in for a count of four and out for a count of six. Placing a hand on the belly and feeling it rise and fall can help keep attention on the breath.

Breathing exercises are a short-term calming tool. If anxiety is frequent or overwhelming, talking with a doctor, counsellor, or trusted person is a good next step.
