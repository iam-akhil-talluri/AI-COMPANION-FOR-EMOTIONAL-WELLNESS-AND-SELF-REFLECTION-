# Understanding and Naming Emotions

Putting a name to a feeling, sometimes called affect labelling, can reduce its intensity and make it easier to decide what you need.

Try going beyond broad words like good or bad. For example, bad might really be disappointed, embarrassed, lonely, frustrated, or tired.

Emotions often carry information: anger can signal that a boundary has been crossed, sadness can signal loss, and anxiety can signal that something feels uncertain or important.
