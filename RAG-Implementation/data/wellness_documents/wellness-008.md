# Reaching Out for Support

Talking to someone you trust, such as a friend, family member, teacher, or colleague, can lighten the weight of difficult feelings. You do not need to have the right words; saying that you are having a hard time is enough to start.

Professional support, such as a doctor, counsellor, or therapist, can help when difficult feelings last for weeks, get in the way of daily life, or feel too big to manage alone.

An AI companion can support reflection, but it is not a replacement for professional care or for people in your life.
