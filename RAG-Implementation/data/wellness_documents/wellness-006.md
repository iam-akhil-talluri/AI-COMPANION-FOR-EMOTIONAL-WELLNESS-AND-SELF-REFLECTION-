# Loneliness and Connection

Feeling lonely is a common human experience and does not mean something is wrong with you. Loneliness is about the quality of connection, not just the number of people around you.

Small steps can help: sending a message to someone you have not spoken to in a while, joining a club, class, or group built around an interest, volunteering, or spending time in shared spaces like libraries or parks.

Reaching out can feel uncomfortable at first. Starting with low-pressure contact, such as a short message or a shared activity, often makes it easier.
