# Practising Self-Compassion

Self-compassion means treating yourself with the same kindness you would offer a friend who is struggling. It is linked with lower stress and greater resilience.

Three parts of self-compassion: noticing that you are having a hard time, remembering that struggling is part of being human, and offering yourself a kind word or gesture.

When you notice harsh self-talk, try asking: Would I say this to someone I care about? What would be a kinder and more accurate way to put it?
